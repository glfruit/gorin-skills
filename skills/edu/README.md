# Edu Skills

Teaching-product skills for textbooks, courses, training handouts, courseware, review packages, and supporting resources.

All skills in this directory use the `gorin-edu-*` prefix.

## Visual And Asset Skills

- [`gorin-edu-chapter-illustrator`](./gorin-edu-chapter-illustrator/) - chapter-level figure planning, insertion points, prompts, captions, and QA evidence.
- [`gorin-edu-diagram-designer`](./gorin-edu-diagram-designer/) - workflow, architecture, state-machine, data-flow, structure, timeline, and comparison diagrams.
- [`gorin-edu-infographic-designer`](./gorin-edu-infographic-designer/) - educational infographics, knowledge cards, visual summaries, and route maps.
- [`gorin-edu-image-optimizer`](./gorin-edu-image-optimizer/) - image derivatives, compression, preservation of originals, and optimization reports.

## Source And Structure Skills

- [`gorin-edu-markdown-polisher`](./gorin-edu-markdown-polisher/) - Markdown source formatting without content rewrite.
- [`gorin-edu-outline-review`](./gorin-edu-outline-review/) - outline conformance review against textbook, course, or training outlines.
- [`gorin-edu-xlsx`](./gorin-edu-xlsx/) - education outline spreadsheet identification, extraction, editing, and validation.

## Delivery And Collaboration Skills

- [`gorin-edu-dispatch-builder`](./gorin-edu-dispatch-builder/) - bounded worker dispatch brief construction.
- [`gorin-edu-docx-review`](./gorin-edu-docx-review/) - DOCX publishing and structure review.
- [`gorin-edu-pptx`](./gorin-edu-pptx/) - editable teaching slide deck creation and editing.

## Organization Rule

Do not place new Edu Team skills under `openclaw/`. New teaching-domain skills belong here, and third-party inspirations should be adapted into `gorin-edu-*` skills rather than used directly.
